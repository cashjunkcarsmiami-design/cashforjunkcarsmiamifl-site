Selling a car from a repair shop or shared parking

At a repair shop

Ask the shop who handles release, where the keys are and whether any parts are stored separately. Resolve the shop’s release requirements directly with its staff. A purchase inquiry does not authorize someone to remove a vehicle from the shop.

In shared parking

Identify the space and the person who can provide entry. Mention a blocked driveway, gate or another vehicle in the access route. Confirm permission with the responsible property contact before agreeing to a collection time.

With unfinished repairs

Separate installed components from loose replacement parts. Tell us whether the wheels roll and whether the steering is usable; an engine that will not start is a different condition from a car that cannot roll.
