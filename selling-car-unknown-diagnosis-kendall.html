Selling a Non-Running Car Without a Confirmed Diagnosis in Kendall

Cash for Junk Cars Miami FL · October 29, 2026

You can describe a car accurately without knowing the exact cause of its problem. When requesting a Kendall junk car offer, separate the symptoms you observed from repair guesses and information that is no longer current.

Start with what you actually know

State the last time the car ran and what happened when you last tried to use it. Describe an observed sound, warning light or failure to start without assigning a cause you have not confirmed. If a shop inspected it, explain when that happened and distinguish its findings from an informal suggestion. Label older videos as older evidence.

Explain the present collection condition

A diagnosis and a pickup plan answer different questions. Tell the buyer whether the car is accessible, whether keys are available and whether the wheels and steering are known to move. If you have not checked something, say it is unknown. Do not repeatedly start, push or test-drive a vehicle simply to complete an inquiry.

Share useful records with context

A written estimate can help explain what was considered, but an estimate is not proof that the work was completed. Identify repairs actually performed and parts removed or replaced. Share relevant vehicle information and avoid including unrelated personal details in photos of paperwork. The buyer can ask for clarification if more information is needed.

Ask for an offer based on the uncertainty

Request terms for the car as it stands, including what will need to be checked before the amount is final. Confirm payment, documents and collection arrangements before scheduling. If a new diagnosis or repair changes the car after the discussion, update the buyer. A clear unknown is more useful than presenting a guess as a confirmed mechanical fact.
