How to Sell a Non-Running Car in Miami

By Cash for Junk Cars Miami FL · September 25, 2026

A car that will not start can take up space while you decide what to do next. Before requesting a cash offer, gather a few details about the vehicle and its location. This helps a buyer evaluate the car and plan collection without guessing about its condition.

This guide is for owners selling a complete vehicle in Miami-Dade, including Hialeah, Kendall and Cutler Bay. Lee el resumen en español.

1. Describe more than “it does not run”

Start with the year, make and model, then explain what happens when you try to start it. Does the engine turn over? Has the car been parked for months? Was it in a collision? Share what you know without trying to diagnose a problem you have not had checked.

  • Say whether the engine starts and whether the vehicle moves.
  • Mention missing components, visible damage and flat tires.
  • Tell the buyer whether you have keys and available ownership documents.
  • Give the city or ZIP code where the car is parked.

A clear description gives the buyer a better starting point. It also helps avoid an offer based on a running vehicle when the car actually needs special collection arrangements.

2. Take photos that show the whole vehicle

Photograph the front, rear, both sides and interior. Include close-ups of collision damage or missing parts. If the car is blocked in, send a photo showing the surrounding space as well. Use recent photos so the offer reflects its current condition.

There is no need to move a vehicle that cannot be moved safely just to improve a picture. Explain any side you cannot reach. You can send vehicle photos on WhatsApp and include the year, make and model in your message.

3. Compare the full offer and pickup arrangements

The offer depends on the vehicle's details, condition and completeness. Two cars of the same model can receive different offers if one has major damage or missing components. Ask what information the buyer used to prepare the quote and whether anything remains to be confirmed.

Before accepting, confirm the purchase amount, payment arrangements and whether any collection costs apply. If an offer changes, ask for the reason before deciding. Do not compare only the largest number without understanding the terms attached to it.

4. Explain the pickup location and access

In a Hialeah driveway, a Kendall apartment parking lot or a Cutler Bay garage, the important details are practical: can the truck reach the vehicle, do the wheels roll, and is another car blocking the way? Tell us about gates, height restrictions and narrow entrances before arranging a time.

If the vehicle is at a repair shop or storage facility, coordinate access and release with that location. Share the exact pickup address when making arrangements. See our Miami-Dade service areas for the communities we cover.

5. Get ready before collection

Check the trunk, glove compartment, door pockets and under the seats for personal belongings. Have the keys and the documents requested for your particular sale ready. If you do not have the title, explain that before scheduling; do not assume every vehicle can be accepted with the same paperwork.

Keep the agreed offer and collection details available. Confirm who will be present and how to reach each other if access or timing changes.
