Comparing a sale with another repair

Describe the latest breakdown

State whether the car currently starts, runs intermittently or no longer moves. Keep a repair completed months ago separate from a fault that is still unresolved. If you do not know the cause, describe the symptom.

Compare complete figures

Use a current repair estimate and a vehicle offer based on the same condition. Ask what the purchase includes and whether collection costs apply. Money spent on earlier repairs is useful history, but is not a promised addition to a new offer.

Plan gated or apartment access

Explain the parking level, gate arrangements and any limited access window. Confirm with the property contact before treating an available time as a collection appointment.

What to send for an offer

  • Year, make and model, plus any known missing components.
  • Whether it starts and rolls; keys and ownership document availability.
  • Current photos of accessible areas and the pickup city or ZIP code.
  • For collection: the exact location, access restrictions and handoff contact.

A description lets us review your vehicle. Confirm the purchase amount, payment arrangement, required documents and any collection costs before you accept. If you do not have the title, explain which records are available first; not every situation can be accepted.

Can I ask for an offer if the car only starts sometimes?

Yes. Tell us that it starts intermittently and when it last ran. Do not describe it as reliably running or drive it simply to demonstrate the fault. Ask us to review the current condition and collection needs together.
