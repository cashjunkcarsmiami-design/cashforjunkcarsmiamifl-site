Can I sell a car that doesn’t run?

We consider non-running vehicles. Tell us whether the car starts, rolls and has keys, and describe any damage so we can review the pickup requirements and offer.

How much is my junk car worth?

The offer depends on the year, make, model, condition and other vehicle details. Call or send photos for a quote. Confirm the final offer before agreeing to the sale.

What if I don’t have the title?

Tell us what ownership documents you have before arranging pickup. We’ll review the situation and explain what is required. Not every vehicle can be accepted without a title.

Where do you pick up cars?

We coordinate pickups in the Miami area. Send the exact location so we can confirm service availability and collection details for your vehicle.

Do you sell parts or provide towing only?

No. This site is for people selling their vehicles. Pickup is discussed as part of the vehicle purchase; we do not sell used parts or book standalone towing here.
