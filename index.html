BEFORE YOU SELL

A clearer junk car offer starts with the details.

Selling a non-running or damaged car

A car does not have to start for us to consider it. When you request an offer, explain whether the engine runs, the wheels roll and the steering works. Mention collision damage, missing components or a flat tire. These details help us understand both the vehicle and the equipment needed for collection.

For a car parked at a repair shop, apartment or storage facility, confirm who can authorize its release. Share any access restrictions before agreeing on a pickup time.

What affects a cash offer?

The year, make, model, overall condition and completeness of the vehicle help determine its value. An offer for a running car may differ from one with engine damage or missing parts. Send current photos and describe known problems so the discussion reflects the car as it is today.

Before accepting, confirm the purchase amount, payment arrangements and whether any pickup costs apply. Ask for clarification if the vehicle’s condition or the pickup plan changes.

Junk car removal as part of your sale

Our pickup service is coordinated with a vehicle purchase. Give us the city or ZIP code first, then the exact collection address when arranging pickup. Tell us about gates, garage height, narrow driveways or other cars blocking access.

We serve sellers in Miami, Hialeah, Kendall and the other areas listed on this page. This is a car-buying service; we do not sell used auto parts or provide standalone roadside towing through this website.

Prepare for pickup

Gather the keys and available ownership documents. Remove personal belongings and check the glove box, trunk and storage compartments. If the title is missing, tell us before scheduling; acceptance depends on the documents and circumstances, and is not guaranteed.

Keep the vehicle accessible and have the agreed purchase and collection details ready. Request your cash offer with the vehicle information below, or call 305-457-0817.
